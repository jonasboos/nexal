Micro SaaS Functionality:
The Micro SaaS is centered around an intuitive and powerful Dashboard where users can manage, edit, and optimize their PDF documents seamlessly. Users can split PDFs into individual pages or custom page ranges, merge multiple PDFs into a single document, compress files to reduce size without losing quality, rotate pages individually or in bulk, reorder or delete pages effortlessly, and add watermarks or stamps. They can also convert PDFs to other formats such as Word, Excel, or JPEG and vice versa, preview documents directly in the Dashboard with zoom, search, and annotation tools, and utilize batch processing to handle multiple PDFs at once with progress tracking and error reporting for large files. Cloud integration allows uploading and saving files directly from Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive, and all processed PDFs are stored in a history log for easy retrieval, version control, and re-editing. The Free Tier enables processing of up to three PDFs per day, providing access to essential functions like splitting, merging, and compression, while the Paid Tier offers unlimited PDF processing, full access to all features including batch operations, rotation, reordering, deletion, watermarking, format conversion, and cloud storage, along with enhanced workflow options like automatic file naming, template saving, and optional AI-assisted optimization suggestions. The platform is designed for speed, scalability, and maximum user convenience, combining comprehensive PDF functionality with a clean, modern, and user-friendly interface.
